# PpyMare 🌙
Welcome to PpyMare – a game where reality blends with dreams, and every choice you make shapes your journey.

![ppy-nightmare](screenshots/gameplay.gif)

### About the Game 🎮
You wake up in a strange place, surrounded by an aura of mystery and unease. Your goal is to survive and find your way back home. Along your path, you will encounter surreal beings – from bizarre creatures to echoes of the past. It is up to you whether you face these conflicts with force or choose the path of mercy.

### Atmosphere & Immersion 🎧
PpyMare is built to immerse you in its unique world through sound:
* **Music:** The soundtrack is carefully curated to enhance the surreal atmosphere of the game and accompany you through every location. 🎵
* **Sound Effects:** Every battle features distinct sound effects that intensify the tension of each encounter. ⚔️
* **Tactile Experience:** As you read through the story, you'll hear the crisp, rhythmic sound of keyboard typing, making the narrative feel more personal. ⌨️

### Gameplay Mechanics ⚙️
* **Fight or Spare:** You can attack enemies to boost your stats, or try to show mercy to reach a different outcome. 🛡️
* **Inventory:** Collect and use items (eggs, leaves, feathers) to heal yourself, strengthen your attacks, or improve your chances of success. 🥇
* **Multiple Endings:** Your actions define your destiny. Whether you choose the path of violence or compassion will lead you to one of the game's unique finales. 🏆
